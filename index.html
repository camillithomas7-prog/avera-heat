Questions

Good to know

How does Heat One warm a room so fast?

A PTC ceramic core heats up the moment it's switched on and a quiet fan spreads the warm air evenly, so the whole room reaches a comfortable temperature within minutes.

Do I need to install anything?

No. There's no wiring, no vents and no tools. Plug it into a standard UK socket, choose your setting and you're done. A wall-mount kit is included if you'd like it off the floor.

Is it safe with children and pets?

Yes. It has overheat protection and an automatic shut-off, and the outer casing stays cool to the touch.

How big a room can it heat?

Rooms up to around 50 m², depending on insulation and layout – ideal for bedrooms, home offices, living rooms and flats.

Does it cool in summer too?

Yes. Switch to fan mode for a refreshing breeze on warm days – one device for the whole year.

What if it's not right for me?

You have 30 days to try it at home. If you're not happy, send it back for a full refund. Every unit is also covered by a 1-year warranty.
